Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?

Pain levels vary greatly depending on the modification. Piercings are typically brief, sharp pain. Scarification and implants involve longer sessions but are manageable with proper preparation. Sclera staining requires anaesthetic. Everyone's pain tolerance is different.

How do you care for modifications?

Each modification has specific aftercare requirements. Generally: keep clean, avoid touching with dirty hands, use saline solution for cleaning, avoid swimming during healing, and follow your artist's specific instructions. Healing times range from weeks to months.

Can modifications be reversed?

Some can, some can't. Piercings close (though stretched lobes may not fully recover). Tattoos can be removed with laser treatment. Sclera staining, scarification, and implant removal may leave permanent marks. Always consider permanence before proceeding.

How do I find a reputable artist?

Research extensively. Check portfolios, read reviews, visit studios in person. Look for proper licensing, sterile equipment, and artists with specific experience in your desired modification. Don't choose based on price—safety and expertise are paramount.

What about employment and social stigma?

This is a real consideration. While attitudes are changing, visible modifications can affect employment prospects in certain industries. I've been fortunate to work in tech where individuality is more accepted. Consider your personal and professional goals before getting visible mods.
