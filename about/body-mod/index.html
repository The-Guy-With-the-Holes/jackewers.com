My Journey

For over two decades, I've been exploring the art and science of body modification. What started as a single ear piercing (in what is now removed helix tissue) has evolved into a lifelong passion for transformation, self-expression, and pushing the boundaries of what the human body can become.

From sclera staining to subdermal implants, from a bifurcated tongue to extensive stretching each modification tells a story and represents a deliberate choice to shape my body.

"Transformation, whether it's code or body, is about pushing boundaries and making intentional choices."

Let me be clear, when I talk about making intentional choices, I mean that every modification is a conscious decision. That's not to say that I haven't made mistakes or gotten the occasional silly tattoo (looking at you 2014 "Shopping list: milk, bread, anal beads, cocaine"), but even those choices were made with intention and a sense of humor.

Philosophy & Approach

Research First

Every modification begins with extensive research. Understanding the risks, healing process, long-term implications, and finding the right professional is crucial.

Professional Artists

I only work with experienced, reputable body modification artists who prioritize safety, sterility, and proper technique. Your body deserves the best.

Healing & Care

Proper aftercare is essential. Following healing protocols, maintaining cleanliness, and being patient during the healing process ensures the best outcomes.

Jewelry Tips

  • Material Matters: Use implant-grade titanium, niobium, or solid gold especially during healing.
  • Proper Sizing: Jewelry that's too small or too large can cause complications. Get professionally fitted.
  • Quality Over Cost: Cheap jewelry can lead to irritation, allergic reactions, and healing issues.
  • Regular Cleaning: Keep jewelry clean to prevent buildup and maintain optimal healing.

Important Information

Body modification is serious. While I'm passionate about my modifications, I want to be clear about some important points:

  • Do Your Research: Understand the procedure, risks, healing time, and long-term implications before committing.
  • Find Professionals: Only work with licensed, experienced body modification artists with proper credentials and portfolios.
  • Safety First: Ensure proper sterilization, use of new needles, and adherence to health regulations.
  • Know the Risks: All modifications carry risks including infection, scarring, and complications.
  • Consider Impact: Think about how modifications might affect employment, social situations, and future decisions.
  • Mental Health: Ensure you're making decisions from a healthy mental state, not as a response to crisis.

This is my journey and my choice. Your body, your choice but make it an informed, safe one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?

Pain is subjective and varies by procedure, location, and individual pain tolerance. Most modifications involve some discomfort, but it's temporary. The healing process often requires more patience than the initial procedure.

Do you regret any of them?

No regrets. Each modification represents a moment in my journey and a conscious decision. Even if my aesthetic preferences evolve, these modifications are part of who I am.

How do you handle professional settings?

I'm fortunate to work in tech where body modifications are generally more accepted. I'm open about my modifications and believe that competence matters more than appearance. The right workplace will value your skills and respect your choices.

Can I ask you questions about body mods?

Absolutely! I'm happy to share my experiences and knowledge. However, I'm not a professional body modification artist I can share my perspective, but always consult with professionals for medical and procedural advice.

Where should I start if I'm interested?

Start small. Get a simple piercing from a reputable piercer. Experience the process, learn about healing and aftercare. As you become comfortable, you can explore more. Never rush into extreme modifications.
